## 3. Placing an order

An order is a request, not a confirmed contract. The contract forms when we
accept the order and assign it for collection. We may decline an order — for
example where we do not cover your area, where no agent is available, or where
the items fall outside what we accept (§4).

Prices are shown in the app before you confirm, in Nigerian Naira, and include
[VAT: STATE WHETHER INCLUSIVE OR EXCLUSIVE]. The price you see at confirmation is
what you pay, except where item counts differ from what you declared — in which

### Address and location

You must provide a precise pickup location by dropping a pin on the map. Our
dispatch works on distance from that pin. An inaccurate pin may cause delay,
failed collection, or cancellation, and repeated failed collections caused by an
inaccurate address may be charged at [FAILED COLLECTION FEE].

## 4. Items we accept

We clean everyday garments and household textiles. We do **not** accept, and
will return uncleaned:

- items containing money, jewellery, or other valuables — **check pockets before
  handing over; we are not liable for items left in pockets**
- wedding dresses, fur, leather, suede, or heavily beaded garments, unless
  agreed in advance in writing
- items contaminated with hazardous, biological or corrosive substances
- items whose care label has been removed or is illegible, where we cannot
  determine a safe cleaning method

You confirm that items you hand over are yours, or that you are authorised to
send them.

### Handoff codes

to the rider at collection; you give a code again at delivery. **Do not share a
code except with the rider actually in front of you.** A completed handover
recorded against a valid code is treated as evidence that the handover happened.

In outline, we collect:

- **Account data** — name, email, phone number.
